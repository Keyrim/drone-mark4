# Fonts

Vendored as released upstream, unmodified, both under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (the license files sit next to the fonts):

- JetBrains Mono 2.304, Regular / Medium / Bold:
  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/releases/tag/v2.304
  (`LICENSE-JetBrainsMono.txt`). Numbers and identifiers of the overlay.
- Inter 4.1, Regular / Medium / SemiBold:
  https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1 (`LICENSE-Inter.txt`).
  Labels and titles of the overlay.

Godot imports the `.ttf` files as `FontFile` resources on its own.
